To generate an accurate HaldCLUT file, our software needs to compare two photos of the exact same scene: the target picture (the look you want to clone) and a neutral reference picture (the baseline). If you want to capture the look of a specific film stock to contribute to our project, please follow these guidelines:

* 📸 Use RAW Mode: If you are using a digital camera or smartphone for the reference photo, RAW mode is highly preferred. This prevents the camera's internal software from adding forced contrast or color shifts.
* 📊 Use a Color Chart (Highly Recommended): For the absolute best results, include a printed or physical color chart (like an X-Rite/Calibrite passport) in the center of both frames.
* 🔒 Lock Your Settings: Turn off Auto White Balance (AWB) and Auto Exposure on your digital camera or phone. Keep the settings fixed so they match the natural lighting of the film shot.
* 🧍 Keep it Static: Avoid moving subjects (like traffic or swaying trees) and shoot from the exact same angle. The two images must align as perfectly as possible.
Thank you! Your submissions directly help us provide more accurate, high-quality HaldCLUTs to creators around the world.
